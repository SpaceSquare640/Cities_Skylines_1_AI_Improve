[h1]2026-09-09 - Rerouting actually happens now, and a week of audit fixes[/h1]

Most of this came from measuring the mod against real sessions instead of reasoning about it. Several things it claimed to do turned out not to be happening at all.

[h2]The big one[/h2]

[b]Congestion rerouting was almost never triggering.[/b] The threshold for "the road ahead is congested enough to look for another way" was set to 80. Measured across 187,000 samples of real play, road congestion reaches that level about 4.6% of the time - so the feature this mod is built around was firing on a twentieth of the occasions it should have. The threshold is now 50, which is the top 10-20% of congestion for nearly every vehicle type, chosen from the measured distribution rather than from a guess. One session went from a handful of reroutes to 17,377, of which 99% found a new path.

If you have ever watched a queue of vehicles sit in a jam with a clear road one block over and wondered why nothing in this mod reacted, that is why.

[h2]Fixed[/h2]

[list]
[*] [b]Loading a second city without restarting the game inherited the first city's state.[/b] Everything this mod remembers is keyed by building or vehicle ID, and the game hands those IDs out again from scratch when a save loads. Nothing was clearing them between saves, so city B could begin with city A's airport occupancy, burning buildings, saturated stations and in-progress reroutes attached to IDs that now meant something completely different - an airport "full" with no planes in it, fire trucks capped against a fire in a city you had left. All of it is cleared on unload now.
[*] [b]A train station could permanently stop receiving intercity trains.[/b] Its "this station is saturated" reading was only ever refreshed by a train pathing toward it, and that same reading was used to refuse spawning trains toward it. Once a station was flagged, the only thing that could clear the flag was the thing being suppressed. Readings now expire on their own.
[*] [b]Passenger capacity is no longer changed at all - by anything in this mod.[/b] Multiplying a vehicle's capacity applies to whatever number is already in that field, which is not necessarily the game's: with Advanced Vehicle Options installed the two multiplied together, and early testing produced a minibus seating 5,000. Every line of capacity-changing code has been removed, not merely switched off. The passenger helicopter capacity boost is gone with it.
[*] [b]Intercity buses can arrive already carrying passengers[/b] (off by default), expressed as a percentage of the vehicle's own real capacity. This replaces the old capacity boost and is the safe way to get the same "buses arrive full" effect: it cannot produce an absurd number, because its ceiling is the vehicle's own capacity, and it works with Advanced Vehicle Options rather than overriding it.
[*] [b]Citizens were effectively assigned one travel mode for life.[/b] The roll that picks walking, driving, taxi or transit was seeded on the citizen's ID, which never changes - so the same citizen got the same answer for every trip they ever took. Build a metro line and the citizens who came up "drive" would never once try it. Only affects Custom transport mode weights, which is off by default.
[*] [b]With TM:PE installed, the emergency vehicle path priority silently did nothing[/b] while the log reported that it had applied successfully. TM:PE replaces the pathfinder this fallback attaches to, so the patched code was never reached. It is no longer registered when TM:PE is present, and says so. Emergency priority still works there through the other, pathfinder-independent patch.
[*] [b]The "scan for empty vehicles" button froze the game[/b] for anyone using More Vehicles, and read the vehicle list from the wrong thread while the simulation was writing to it.
[*] [b]The settings page could become unreachable.[/b] Searching, then reopening the page at a window too narrow to draw the search box, left it in search mode with the navigation hidden and no box to clear the search - every setting out of reach for the rest of the session. Search results now carry a "clear search" button, the page can no longer be left in that state, search matches section names as well as setting names, and the sections have a scrollbar.
[*] [b]Preset buttons no longer clip their own labels[/b] in German, French, Spanish and Russian.
[*] [b]Garbage trucks and hearses bunch up considerably less.[/b] The cap added on 2026-09-05 limited how many vehicles could be on one building at once, but not how often the same nearest building won the next one - 73% of redirects still went to a building already at half the cap. Distance is now weighted by how many vehicles are already heading there, and that figure dropped to 26%.
[/list]

[h2]Changed[/h2]

[list]
[*] [b]The numeric sliders are hidden by default.[/b] These values are calibrated against measurements from real play, and changing them can stop a feature working with no visible sign that anything is wrong - the reroute threshold above is exactly that failure. Every feature's on/off switch is unchanged and still where it was; the numbers now sit behind [i]Show advanced tuning values[/i] on the General page. Existing settings files have their numeric values reset once to this version's calibrated defaults, because a wrong number that lives in a slider cannot be corrected by changing a default - anyone who had opened the panel already had the old number saved.
[*] [b]Aircraft rerouting has moved to a new Experimental section and is switched off.[/b] Measured across 5,000 samples, taxiway congestion never exceeded 10.8 against a threshold of 50, so it cannot fire and no threshold value would change that - it needs a different signal altogether. It is left visible and labelled as not working rather than quietly deleted, because a switch that silently does nothing is worse than one that says so.
[/list]

[h2]Known, and being worked on[/h2]

[b]Intercity train spawn rate.[/b] On a reported city the game attempts to bring an intercity train in 1,760 times over half an hour and succeeds 100 of them - a 5.7% success rate, against 29-70% for every other transport mode on the same map. This is not caused by this mod: the spawn throttle that was supposed to influence it turned out to be attached to a mechanism the game does not use for intercity trains, and had never run once. Diagnostics are in place and the cause is being narrowed down.

[h1]2026-09-06 - Chinese players finally get the Chinese settings page[/h1]

[list]
[*] [b]Fixed the interface staying English for anyone playing the game in Chinese.[/b] Language detection was matching against codes the game does not actually use: it ships one Chinese locale called "zh", while this mod's tables were named "zh-tw" and "zh-cn", so the lookup missed every time and quietly fell back to English. With the language dropdown on Auto - the default - the two Chinese translations this mod ships were unreachable. "zh" now resolves to Simplified Chinese, and picking Traditional explicitly in the dropdown still works as before. Language matching is also more forgiving in general now, so a region-specific code the game might report in future finds the right table instead of dropping to English.
[*] [b]Performance work on the busiest code in the mod.[/b] A companion-mod lookup that scanned every loaded assembly was running twice per train per tick with no caching, ahead of the very check meant to make that path cheap; it is cached now and runs after that check. The same reordering was applied to the settings reads in the road-vehicle reroute path, the busiest hook here. And cargo trucks were having their reroute check run twice each tick because of a duplicate registration added on a mistaken assumption.
[/list]

[h1]2026-09-05 - Two player-reported fixes: vanishing planes, and sanitation vehicles bunching up[/h1]

Both reported by Fey Warrior, who had turned off stand selection and the sanitation features to work around them. Both are safe to turn back on.

[list]
[*] [b]Fixed planes appearing, taxiing to a stand, vanishing, and repeating.[/b] When an airport was judged full this mod refused the landing, and the game responds to a refused landing by unspawning the plane - but unspawning a vehicle is not the same as releasing it, so the tracking that says "this stand is taken" was never cleared for those planes. An airport's occupancy could therefore only ever grow: each refusal left a permanent mark, which made the airport look fuller, which refused more planes. Left long enough the airport never accepted anything again. Occupancy is now cleared whenever this mod is the reason a vehicle goes away, and a live session confirms it rising and falling normally instead of climbing. The same leak applied to emergency helicopters grounded during a thunderstorm, where a grounded fire helicopter could hold a responder slot at a burning building indefinitely - also fixed.
[*] [b]Fixed garbage trucks and hearses converging on one building.[/b] Two causes. There was no limit on how many idle vehicles this mod would redirect to the same building, so every idle truck in the city picked the same nearest candidate; there is now a cap of four. And hearses were treating cemeteries as somewhere that still needed collecting, because the game reports a cemetery's [i]stored[/i] body count rather than any amount awaiting pickup - so a cemetery always looked like it needed a hearse, and being centrally placed it usually won on distance. Cemeteries and landfills are now excluded from this search entirely. This is what made the target "not necessarily full - in many cases empty".
[/list]

[h1]2026-09-05 - Airports and emergency helicopters no longer stay shut after a storm[/h1]

[b]Fixed thunderstorm shutdowns outlasting the thunderstorm.[/b] A storm that has stopped moves into a cleanup state that lasts a long time, and the check for "is a thunderstorm happening" was still counting that state as an active storm. Measured in one session: 5,929 refused landings and grounded emergency helicopters across 16 continuous minutes with no storm in progress, ending only because the session did. Because fire helicopters are grounded along with the other emergency helicopters, [b]this could again leave burning buildings with no response at all[/b] - the same symptom as the fix on 2026-08-17, one state further along in the game's own disaster sequence. Airports and emergency helicopters now resume the moment the weather itself stops, rather than waiting for cleanup to finish.

[h1]2026-09-04 - Real Time supported, and a clearer answer on citizen transport odds[/h1]

[b]Real Time is supported alongside AI_Improve[/b] - optional, not required, and nothing needs turning off on either side. Decompiling it confirmed the two mods never touch the same code: Real Time decides [i]when[/i] a citizen sets off (scheduling, health, state), AI_Improve's citizen features decide [i]what they travel in[/i]. Both work exactly as they would alone.

[b]New: the log now records which companion mods were detected[/b] - Real Time, Transfer Manager CE, SingleTrainTrackAI, Reversible Tram AI and Advanced Vehicle Options - as a single line at startup. Nothing in a bug report's log used to say which of these were installed, which is often the first thing worth knowing.

[b]Documented: how AI_Improve interacts with mods that customize citizen transport probabilities[/b] (such as Lifecycle Rebalance Revisited). It depends on which Citizens settings you have on, and that was never written down anywhere: [b]Citizen car probability[/b] and [b]Citizen taxi probability[/b] (both on by default) read whatever value the other mod produced and adjust it from there, so the two stack. [b]Custom transport mode weights[/b] (off by default) replaces vanilla's whole vehicle-choice step instead, so the probability methods those mods customize are never called - turning it on overrides them completely, and no citizen cycles while it is on. The setting's own description now says so in all nine languages, and the wiki's Compatibility page covers it.

[h1]2026-08-20 - Garbage trucks and hearses stop waiting on distant work[/h1]

[b]New: idle garbage trucks/hearses seek nearby buildings[/b] (on by default, new "Sanitation" section). A truck or hearse that still has room checks for a nearby building that genuinely still needs it first, instead of only posting an offer and waiting on the game's own priority/distance-*weighted* matching - which is not the same as nearest-first, and can leave a truck idling while a much closer building with the same need goes unserved. Mid-route congestion rerouting was already covered for these vehicles (they're ordinary road vehicles); this closes the other real gap.

[h1]2026-08-17 - Settings page rebuilt, and an important fire response fix[/h1]

[h2]Settings page rebuilt[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Every feature is now a card[/b] holding its own switch and its own sliders together - the old design put a section's switches on one tab and its numbers on a different tab, so you could never see both at once for the same feature.

[h2]Important fire response fix[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Fixed emergency helicopters being grounded (and airports closed) indefinitely after a thunderstorm ended.[/b] The check for "is a thunderstorm happening" only tested whether a disaster record still existed, not whether it was still running - and a finished storm's record lingers long after the weather clears. Measured in one 62-minute session: emergency helicopters were refused dispatch in 55 of those minutes continuously and airports refused over 13,000 landings. Because fire helicopters are grounded along with the other emergency helicopters, [b]this is why burning buildings could get no response at all[/b]. It now uses the same test the game itself uses, so a storm only counts while it is genuinely in progress.
[*] [b]Fixed a growing slowdown around fire dispatch.[/b] The list of known burning buildings was never cleaned up - every building that had ever caught fire stayed in it for the rest of the session, and that whole list is re-scanned every time a fire vehicle looks for another fire to attend. It now drops buildings once their fire is out.
[*] [b]Much smaller logs.[/b] Several per-vehicle diagnostics were still writing to the log even with Verbose logging turned off. In one player's session those accounted for 90% of the mod's entire log output. They now respect the Verbose logging switch like everything else.
[/list]
