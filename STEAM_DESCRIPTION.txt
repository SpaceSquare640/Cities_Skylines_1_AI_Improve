[h1]AI_Improve[/h1]

A Cities: Skylines (2015) mod that improves the game's traffic, citizen, and service vehicle AI decision quality - by SpaceSquare.

[h2]Why this mod exists[/h2]

Most existing "improved AI" mods for Cities: Skylines change one narrow slice of behavior (a single tweak to one method, one constant) and the effect is barely noticeable in an actual playthrough. This project targets a gap those mods leave open: they all optimize a decision once, at the moment it's made, and never revisit it as conditions change. AI_Improve focuses on [b]continuous, dynamic adjustment[/b] instead - vehicles and citizens that reconsider their decisions as the city around them changes, not just once at spawn time.

[h2]What it improves[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Emergency vehicles[/b]: path-cost-ignoring dispatch, a real per-building responder cap, idle vehicles that check for nearby fires first. Incorporates and extends [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2346565561]Smarter Firefighters[/url] - no need to subscribe to it separately.
[*] [b]Trains, intercity trains, and metro[/b]: occupancy-aware platform assignment, mid-journey rerouting, throughput-aware spawn throttling.
[*] [b]Aircraft[/b]: occupancy-aware gate assignment, mid-flight rerouting, thunderstorm response (airports refuse landings and departures, emergency helicopters stay grounded, for the duration of the storm).
[*] [b]Buses, passenger helicopters[/b]: dynamic rerouting, boosted helicopter capacity.
[*] [b]Ordinary city traffic[/b]: every road vehicle reroutes around real-time congestion mid-journey, not just once at trip start.
[*] [b]Garbage trucks and hearses[/b]: an idle vehicle with room to spare seeks a nearby building that still needs it, instead of only waiting on the game's own priority/distance-weighted offer matching.
[*] [b]Cargo ships and passenger ferries[/b]: occupancy-aware dock assignment, spreading harbor traffic across real dock capacity instead of piling onto the same one.
[*] [b]Citizens[/b]: less likely to drive into already-congested destinations; taxi usage boosted as the alternative. Optional [b]Citizen Transport AI[/b] (off by default): 4 weight sliders - Walk, Drive, Taxi, Transit - fully replace vanilla's fixed age-based odds, with 4 one-click presets (Balanced, Transit-oriented, Car-dependent, Walkable city).
[*] [b]Race cars[/b]: racetrack building attractiveness boost. (Racer speed itself is left fully vanilla - an earlier top-speed override caused racers to lose control and has been removed.)
[/list]

[b]See the [url=https://github.com/SpaceSquare640/Cities_Skylines_1_AI_Improve/wiki]GitHub Wiki[/url] for the full, detailed feature list and compatibility notes[/b] (soft dependencies/integrations with TM:PE, Transfer Manager CE, Smarter Firefighters, SingleTrainTrackAI, Reversible Tram AI, Advanced Vehicle Options).

[h2]Settings[/h2]

Every individual feature - not just broad categories - is its own card with its own switch and its own sliders together, description text included, not hidden behind a tooltip.

[list]
[*] [b]Full settings[/b]: ESC → Options → Content Manager → Mods → AI_Improve. Eleven sections down the left, grouped by transport mode (General, Citizens, Emergency, Sanitation, Road Traffic, Rail, Aviation, Shipping & Cargo, Races, Advanced, About). A search box at the top filters every setting across every section at once. 25 independent feature toggles and 28 adjustable sliders in total - fire responders per building, reroute density thresholds, gate/platform/dock capacities, citizen car/taxi/walk/transit behavior, and more. A "Reset to defaults" button restores everything (except your language choice) if you tune yourself into a corner.
[*] [b]Quick panel[/b]: the AI_Improve button on the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2966990700]UnifiedUI[/url] toolbar, in-game. Tools to find and remove empty intercity trains/buses without leaving your city - full toggle/tuning control lives in Content Manager. Optional - without UnifiedUI, Content Manager is still the only way in.
[/list]

Interface language follows the game automatically, or pick English, 繁體中文 or 简体中文 from the language dropdown. Each is listed in its own script, so you can find yours even if the current interface language is unreadable to you.

Turning a feature off makes it behave exactly as if it had never been written - the game's own original code runs untouched.

[b]Full settings reference: [url=https://github.com/SpaceSquare640/Cities_Skylines_1_AI_Improve/wiki/Settings]Settings wiki page[/url][/b]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] Cities: Skylines (base game)
[*] Harmony (Mod Dependency) by boformer, subscribed via Steam Workshop
[*] Optional: UnifiedUI - for the in-game settings button
[/list]

[h2]Reporting a problem[/h2]

Please open an issue on [url=https://github.com/SpaceSquare640/Cities_Skylines_1_AI_Improve/issues/new/choose]GitHub[/url] and attach your full Cities_Data/output_log.txt - almost nothing can be diagnosed without it. Turn on [b]Verbose logging[/b] (settings → General) before reproducing the problem, and include the "Updated" line shown in the settings header - it identifies the exact code your build came from.
